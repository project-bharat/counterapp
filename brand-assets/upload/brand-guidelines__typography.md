# Bilan typography

## Wordmark
The "Bilan" wordmark and the B monogram are **custom lettering supplied as artwork**. They are not set in a font.
Always place the supplied SVG or PNG files. Never retype the name in a font and never redraw it.

## Companion fonts (for product UI and documents)
| Role | Font | Weights | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / headlines | Cormorant Garamond | Light 300, Regular 400, Medium 500 | Headlines at 24 sp and above. Hairlines break down below that. |
| Interface / body | Inter | Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600 | Body text, labels, buttons, numerals, tables |

Both fonts were chosen to sit next to a high-contrast serif wordmark: a refined serif for large moments and a neutral sans for everything functional. They are recommendations, not part of the supplied artwork, so change them if the brand owner prefers other fonts.

## Suggested type scale (Android, sp / line height)
| Style | Font | Size / line |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Cormorant Garamond Medium | 32 / 40 |
| Title | Inter SemiBold | 20 / 28 |
| Body | Inter Regular | 16 / 24 |
| Label | Inter Medium | 14 / 20 |
| Caption | Inter Regular | 12 / 16 |
